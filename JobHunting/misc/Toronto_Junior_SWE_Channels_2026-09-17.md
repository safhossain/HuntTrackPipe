## A. Agencies you can register with today

### How this actually works for a junior (read once)
- Agencies are paid by the client, not you. Under Ontario's ESA (licensing in force since **July 1, 2024**), recruiters and temp agencies must be licensed and **cannot charge you a fee**. Anyone asking for money is a scam or breaking the law. ([ontario.ca](https://www.ontario.ca/page/licensing-temporary-help-agencies-and-recruiters))
- Toronto agency volume is overwhelmingly **mid/senior contract** at banks, insurers, telcos, and the Ontario Public Service (vendor-of-record). Junior yield is real but low: expect QA, application support, "Solutions Developer I", junior dev at insurers/banks.
- Register once, apply to **specific reqs**, message the **named recruiter**, respond within the hour. Then stop thinking about it. Budget ~10% of effort here.
- Set LinkedIn to "Open to work → recruiters only". That is how most of these firms will find you.
